Privacy & Root Stewardship

Your data is held with the same reverence we hold for ancient oaks. This policy explains how we tend to your information.

Our promise

At Iverstone Verdalis Forum, we believe in transparency, care, and the quiet dignity of every digital root. We collect only what is necessary to nurture the community, and we never sell or trade your personal data. This policy outlines the types of information we gather, how we use it, and the choices you have.

  • We collect minimal data: name, email, and forum activity.
  • Your data is stored securely and accessed only by core stewards.
  • You may request deletion or export of your data at any time.
  • We use cookies only to enhance your experience — never for intrusive tracking.

What we gather

Account data: when you register, we ask for a display name, email address, and optional bio. This helps us recognize you in the living library.

Usage data: we may collect anonymous analytics (like page views) to understand which parts of the forum resonate most. This is aggregated and never tied to your identity.

Messages: if you contact us via the form or direct message, we retain that correspondence to provide support and improve our responses.

How we use it

To cultivate community: your email allows us to send occasional updates about new threads, events, or seasonal gatherings (you can opt out anytime).

To improve: we analyze usage patterns to make the forum more intuitive and to surface the most relevant conversations.

To protect: we may use data to prevent spam, harassment, or other behaviour that threatens the wellbeing of our members.

We never share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies & similar

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and to remember your preferences. We also use a small number of analytics cookies (via our own lightweight tracker) to understand which topics are most alive. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some forum features may become less smooth.

For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

Your rights

Access: you can request a copy of all personal data we hold.

Correction: update or correct any inaccuracies in your profile.

Deletion: ask us to erase your account and associated data (we'll retain only anonymized contributions for archival integrity).

Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these, email us at data@iverstone-verdalis.forum.

Retention rhythm: we keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Inactive accounts are pruned after 24 months, and we anonymise your contributions to preserve the living archive.

Children’s privacy: our forum is not directed at under-13s. If we learn we have collected data from a child without consent, we will delete it promptly.
Policy updates: we may revise this policy; the latest version will always be here, with a revision date.

Last updated: 21 July 2026 — rooted in transparency.
