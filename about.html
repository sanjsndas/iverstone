About the living library

Iverstone Verdalis Forum is a sanctuary for garden narratives, soil wisdom, and the stories that root us to the earth.

A garden of stories

Founded in 2019, the forum began as a small gathering of botanists, historians, and gardeners who believed that every plant carries a memory. Today, we are a global community of storytellers, cultivators, and regenerators.

Our name — Iverstone — echoes the ancient stone walls that hold heat and history. Verdalis is our commitment to the green, the living, the unfolding.

join the forum

Roots

We honour deep time — heirloom seeds, ancient cultivars, and the mycelial networks that connect all living things.

Narratives

Every garden tells a story. We collect, preserve, and share personal essays, oral histories, and botanical folklore.

Regeneration

From soil restoration to permaculture design, we champion practices that heal the land and the communities around it.

More than a forum: the living library

Iverstone Verdalis Forum is a living library — an ever-growing archive of garden knowledge, personal narratives, and ecological wisdom. Unlike a traditional library, our stacks are alive: they breathe, bloom, and change with the seasons.

Our approach to knowledge

We believe that knowledge is not static. It is passed down through generations, whispered between roots, and encoded in the scent of wet earth. That is why we foreground oral histories, indigenous practices, and hands-on experimentation alongside academic research.

Key principles

  • Radical inclusion: All gardeners, from balcony growers to forest farmers, have a place here.
  • Deep listening: We prioritise patience and observation over quick fixes.
  • Intergenerational exchange: We actively connect elders with young growers.
  • Soil as commons: Healthy soil is a shared resource that we must protect together.

Frequently asked questions

How can I contribute?

You can share a garden story, a seed variety, or a soil observation. We also welcome essays, poetry, and artwork.

Is the forum only for experts?

Not at all. We celebrate beginners and seasoned growers alike. Everyone brings a unique perspective.

How do you preserve stories?

Through our digital archive, seed libraries, and an annual printed anthology. We also host listening circles.

Common misconceptions

  • Myth: Gardening is about control. Fact: It is about collaboration with natural systems.
  • Myth: You need a large space. Fact: A windowsill or a community plot is enough.
  • Myth: Soil is inert. Fact: It is the most biodiverse habitat on earth.
